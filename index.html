About the model

  • Model: EfficientNet-B0, pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on MRI scans.
  • Dataset: Epic and CSCR hospital dataset, 12,064 MRI scans in four classes (train and test folders).
  • Image size: every scan is resized to 224 x 224 pixels before the model sees it.
  • Fixes against overfitting: early stopping, augmentation (flips, rotation, brightness), dropout, weight decay, and class weights.
  • Limits: the same patient may appear in more than one split, which can make scores look better than they are.
